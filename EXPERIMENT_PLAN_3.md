- **Decisions:** D1–D7 approved by Edward as recommended (§12).
- **Pilot:** done, and the only permitted adjustment it triggered has been applied (§10).
- **Record:** §13 lists the commits, configs and the exact study command.
- **Where the numbers come from:** every number is copied from `results/exp3_planning/tables.md` (Appendix), which is generated by `analysis/exp3_planning_tables.py`.

## 1. Question

**RQ3.** When the reliability of an *authorized* action class degrades mid-stream, does AMP's authority lifecycle withdraw that authority in time to materially limit wrong autonomous decisions?
- **Compared with:** the same authorization held statically, with the same probes but no consequences.
- **Condition:** it must not withdraw authority when nothing changes.

**Why this and not per-decision accuracy.** Experiments 1–2 and `TARGET_MODEL_REVIEW.md` §3.1 showed the limit of a stationary stream: with score-only features, a well-chosen static threshold is near-optimal. The lifecycle's distinctive claim is governance over time, which only shows under change.

- **H1 (consequences under change).** In the shift arm, AMP makes fewer post-shift wrong autonomous releases ("escapes") than the static twin T1.
- **H2 (no over-reaction under stationarity).** In the stationary arm:
  - AMP rarely revokes the class;
  - AMP's autonomous error on the class stays within the risk budget.

**Not claimed:** budget compliance *under* the shift.
- Probe-only monitoring can bound exposure. It cannot prevent the errors made before the change is detected.
- Planning puts AMP's whole-run error on the class in the shift arm at 0.116 (option B) and 0.146 (option A), both above the 0.10 budget (T5).
- This is reported, and the paper must say it plainly.

## 2. Environment and shift construction (sketch E-A, made concrete)

**Pool.**
- All five LOCO categories, pooled into **one agent**: a multi-product inspection cell.
- The test split only; the same frozen PatchCore scores and calibration as Experiments 1–2.
- **Why pool:** good images are the binding supply. Pooled there are 575; the three shift-sensitive categories alone have 379, too few for a pre-shift qualification period plus a post-shift monitoring period.

**Stream: 630 items per run**, sampled without replacement within a run; seeds permute (T1).

| Segment | Items | Composition |
|---|---|---|
| Pre | 1–380 | good + structural anomalies, structural prevalence 0.10 |
| Post, **shift arm** | 381–630 | good 0.60, structural 0.10, **logical 0.30**: a new failure mode appears on top of the existing one |
| Post, **stationary arm** | 381–630 | the pre regime continues |

**Why this is a reliability change for the class AMP can authorize.**
- The "good" (release) proposals that AMP can authorize are the high-margin ones.
- Missed structural defects sit near the threshold. Missed logical anomalies look normal to PatchCore and land among them.
- In planning, the error of the authorized good slice rises from 0.036 before the shift to 0.243 after it (T7, budget 0.10). The score features cannot see this; only human evidence can.

**AMP is not told about the change.** Nothing is declared and no field changes. The action fields are the same as in Experiments 1–2: `proposal`, `score_z`, `margin_abs`. `anomaly_type`, `ground_truth` and category are never sent.

**Caveats (§11):**
- The shift is constructed.
- Prevalence also rises from 0.10 to 0.40. The shift is "a new failure mode at a high rate", not a pure type swap.
- A weaker shift (logical 0.20) is kept as planning sensitivity only (T4, T5).

## 4. Evidence budgets

- **Stage 0:** 150 human rows. Then qualification on prospective, model-scoped evidence from activation.
- **What certification needs** at budget 0.10 and 95%:
  - a slice of 30–40 rows with no errors, 50–60 rows with at most 1 error, or 100 rows with at most 4 (T2).
- **Planning, option B:**
  - authorization at item q10/q50/q90 = 190 / 210 / 310, i.e. about 60 items of evidence after activation at the median;
  - P(good class authorized before the shift and model accepted) = 0.838 (T4).
- **The defective class is never authorized before the shift** (T6). At 10% prevalence, "defective" proposals are wrong too often. Defects stay human-confirmed, and Experiment 3 is about the release class. This is realistic, but it means the claim "revoke only the affected class" cannot be tested here.
- **Grid floor.** The certified threshold sits at the grid floor, 0.8, in 0.406 of runs in planning (T6).
  - Both pilot runs also certified at 0.8, which triggered ADJ-2 (§10).
  - In both pilots no good proposal had P below 0.80 (q10 ≈ 0.87), so on those streams the extension changes nothing but the label.

## 5. Risk budgets

- **0.10 for both classes** (**D3**). The budget is the maximum share of autonomous releases that are defective.
- **0.05 is infeasible with this evidence supply:** P(eligible) = 0.195 against 0.838 at 0.10 (T7).
- **0.10 is loose for real QA.** It is set by the evidence available (575 good images) and is stated as a limitation, not a recommendation.

## 6. Probe power and the monitor (D1)

**What a probe can detect** (exact, T3). With 20 probes, P(revoke) is:
- 0.585 at a true slice error of 0.25;
- 0.762 at 0.30.

With 40 probes these rise to 0.818 and 0.945.

**The shipped monitor (option A) dilutes change.** It evaluates *all* probes since authorization. After a long clean pre-shift authorized period, the post-shift errors are averaged with that clean history, so the lower bound rises slowly.

| Planning result (T4, T5) | A: shipped monitor | B: recent-window rule |
|---|---|---|
| P(revoked after shift) | 0.427 | 0.818 |
| Median items to revoke | 160 | 100 |
| Post-shift escapes vs T1 | 0.81 | 0.46 |
| False revocations, stationary arm | 0.006 | 0.012 |
| Assurance of the §8 rule at R=12 | 0.789 | 0.965 |
| Assurance with a weaker shift, R=12 | 0.089 | 0.946 |

Neither option costs useful autonomy under stationarity: correct executions are 0.997 of T1's (T5).

**Option B = "P8", a small AMP change.** Also revoke when the one-sided 95% lower bound over the most recent W=20 probes exceeds the budget. It needs at least 10 probes and uses the same Clopper–Pearson test.

- It is a monitor parameter (`monitoring.recent_window`, default off). It would be implemented and gated exactly like P1–P7: three suites plus the legacy replay.
- Repeated windowed testing inflates false alarms; the stationary arm measures that (0.012 in planning).

**Decision (D1): B, approved 2026-09-25; implemented as P8 (§3).** The rationale: the experiment tests whether withdrawal works. Under A, planning already says it is mostly too slow, so running Experiment 3 on A would largely re-measure a known design weakness. That weakness was found by this planning, not by earlier tests, and should be reported as such.

- **Option A** (not chosen) would have tested AMP as shipped, with an expected small effect (ratio 0.81) and 0.789 assurance at R=12.

## 7. Comparators

All are computed offline from the same run's logs. This is exact because the model is frozen: every decision logs P from the one model, and the probe schedule is deterministic.

- **T1, static authorization with probes (PRIMARY):**
  - the same class, certified threshold and time as AMP's *first* authorization;
  - the same probe schedule;
  - **never revoked.**
  - It isolates the value of *consequences*. The difference from AMP is only what revocation, and requalification, changes.
- **T0, static authorization without probes:** conventional static HITL with matched information and matched budget. Every item above the first certified threshold executes. System-level comparator (secondary).
- **T-margin (secondary; the design §7 hook):**
  - the same certification rule applied to the raw margin;
  - on AMP's logged evidence ids (`AUTHORITY_QUALIFICATION_EVALUATED`) at AMP's evaluation times;
  - frozen after its first pass.
  - It tests whether AMP's learned score adds anything over the margin.
- **References:** full HITL, model-only (execute every proposal), and Experiment 1's static δ = 1.0.

## 8. Endpoints and preregistered success rule

**Analysis set.** An arm's runs where the first model was accepted **and** the good class was authorized before item 381.
- Eligibility depends only on pre-shift events, so it cannot be influenced by the post-shift endpoint.
- Ineligible runs are reported, not analysed for H1/H2.

**Primary, H1 (shift arm).**
- E = post-shift escapes: autonomous `good` executions on items 381–630 whose ground truth is defective.
- ρ = ΣE_AMP / ΣE_T1 over eligible shift runs.
- **H1 passes iff the one-sided 95% upper bound of ρ is < 1.** The bound comes from a run-level bootstrap: 10,000 resamples of runs, percentile method.

**H2 (stationary arm), both required:**
- (a) the good class is revoked in ≤ 10% of eligible runs;
- (b) AMP's pooled autonomous error on the good class is ≤ 0.10.

**Success = H1 and H2.** Anything else is reported as "not supported". There are no alternative analyses, reruns or tuning after unblinding.

**Computation, fixed** (`analysis/exp3_analysis.py`; self-test `analysis/test_exp3_analysis.py`):

- **Mapping events to positions.**
  - An authority event maps to the stream position of the tick that produced it (payload `job_id`).
  - An admin-approved authorization maps to the tick whose settle approved its workitem.
  - A change at the tick after item k takes effect from item k+1.
  - Eligibility requires good-class authorization at a tick at or before item 380.
- **Errors.**
  - An *escape* is an autonomous execution of a `good` proposal whose ground truth is defective.
  - A defective-class error is an autonomous execution of a `defective` proposal on a good part.
- **T1 and T0.**
  - Threshold: the first good-class authorization's certified threshold, applied to logged P.
  - Items: those after the authorization position.
  - Probe schedule (T1 only): AMP's counter schedule, `probesDue`, never reset.
  - `frozen_model_ok` requires every scored decision to come from one model version. A run failing it is reported, and its twins are flagged.
- **TM (the margin twin).**
  - Grid (strictest first): `[3.0, 2.5, 2.0, 1.75, 1.5, 1.25, 1.0, 0.75, 0.5, 0.25, 0.0]` on `margin_abs`.
  - Rule and statistics: the same certification rule, budget 0.10, 30 rows, 95%.
  - When it runs: evaluated on the evidence ids of each good-class `AUTHORITY_QUALIFICATION_EVALUATED`, in order.
  - Frozen at its first pass, and effective after that tick.
- **Bootstrap.**
  - B = 10,000 resamples of eligible shift runs; numpy seed 20260925.
  - The ratio of sums per resample, with the conventions 0/0 → 1 and x/0 → ∞.
  - The upper bound is the 95th percentile with the non-interpolating `higher` method.
- **Minimum analysable set.** If an arm has fewer than 5 eligible runs after top-up, the result is "NOT EVALUABLE".

**Rule strictness (D6).** Requiring a halving (ρ ≤ 0.5) was considered. Its assurance is 0.636 at R=12 even under option B (T8), so superiority is recommended, with ρ and its CI reported as the effect size.

**Secondary (descriptive, preregistered):**
- ρ vs T0 and vs T-margin;
- P(revocation) and delay in items after the shift;
- escapes before revocation;
- AMP's whole-run error against the budget, per arm (not claimed);
- useful autonomy (correct executions) and HITL burden, per arm and system;
- the number of probes;
- requalification after revocation;
- the defective class;
- escapes by category;
- the timing of false revocations.

## 9. Sample size and runtime

- **Seeds:** R = 12 fixed stream seeds per arm, 24 runs.
  - **Top-up:** if fewer than 10 runs in an arm are eligible, preregistered extra seeds are added in order until 10 are eligible, with a cap of 16. This is valid because eligibility is pre-shift only.
- **Assurance** of the §8 rule at R=12: 0.965 under option B, 0.789 under option A (T5). Assurance is P(success rule passes) over the planning distribution.
- **Runtime (measured in the pilot):** 19.65 min (shift) and 26.36 min (stationary) per run, so roughly 9–10.5 h for 24 runs.
  - The study driver runs sequentially and stops each run's RLHF worker after the run, per amendment v1.3.
- **Independence:** runs reuse one image pool, so inference is conditional on the pool, as in Experiments 1–2.

## 11. Threats to validity

- **A constructed shift, with a prevalence increase.** Not a natural drift; external validity is limited. MVTec AD 2 (sketch E-B) remains the realistic follow-up.
- **The oracle is ideal:** no reviewer noise or delay.
- **Probe-only monitoring.** Downstream feedback (customer returns) would detect escapes faster. Off by design, to keep the test on AMP's own evidence; it could be a follow-up.
- **The planning replica approximates AMP's model and trainer** (logistic regression proxy, tick and approval timing). Hence the pilot.
- **The model is frozen,** so retrain and model-change requalification (and its autonomy cost) are out of scope.
- **The budget is 0.10** (§5). The defective class stays inert (§4).
- **P8 changed AMP after the design.** It was motivated by planning, implemented before preregistration and gated. It must be disclosed as such in the paper.
